🍴 Recipe Catcher

Clean, ad-free recipes — in your browser.

Grab just the ingredients and instructions from any recipe page — no ads, no pop-ups, no life story. View, print, or save a clean text-only recipe, in metric or imperial.

What it does

  • Pulls the recipe out of the page and opens it in a clean, readable tab
  • One click between metric and imperial — dry goods in grams, liquids in ml
  • Oven temperatures annotated both ways, e.g. 450 degrees F (232°C)
  • Adjust the servings and every quantity rescales
  • A shopping list with tickable checkboxes, and a Copy button
  • Estimated nutrition per serving
  • Print or save a tidy PDF
  • Automatic light and dark mode

Private by design

Recipe Catcher has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and makes no network requests. Everything happens on your device, and nothing is ever uploaded.
